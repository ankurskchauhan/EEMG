Research

Materials for energy, space, nuclear and defense applications.

Energy and space: fatigue and creep

We study how steels and novel alloys, including oxide dispersion-strengthened (ODS) steels, multi-principal element alloys and magnesium alloys, deform and fail under repeated loading at high temperature. Figure source: dislocation pinning in ODS steel (paper).

Related papers

Nuclear: irradiation effects

We examine how neutron and proton irradiation change the microstructure and strength of tungsten, reactor steels and multi-principal element alloys, for example needle-shaped W(Os,Re)3 precipitates and hexagonal cavities in pure tungsten (paper).

Related papers

Defense: shock and impact

We study how metals and ceramics respond to very fast, high-pressure loading, including amorphous shear bands in indented boron carbide (paper).

Related papers

Machine learning for extreme conditions

We build physics-guided models that predict fatigue life, yield strength and fracture toughness of steels and alloys under reactor-relevant conditions.
